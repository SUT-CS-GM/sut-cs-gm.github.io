# Midterm Exam

The Fall 2025 Midterm Exam serves as a comprehensive assessment of the foundational concepts covered in the first half of the semester. The exam is designed to evaluate your theoretical understanding, mathematical derivations, and analysis of model architectures presented from **Topic 1 (Introduction)** through **Topic 8 (Normalizing Flows)**.

---

## Logistics

The exam will take place during the regular class hours.

* **Date:** Sunday, <span dir="rtl" style="font-family: Vazirmatn;">۹ آذر ۱۴۰۴</span> (November 30, 2025)
* **Time:** 10:30 – 12:30
* **Location:** Department of Mathematical Sciences, Classroom 211 *(same as all classes)*

## Scope & Material

The exam covers **Topics 1 through 8**. Please review the slides and notes for the following sections:

1.  [**Topic 1: Introduction**](/material/#topic-1-introduction)
2.  [**Topic 2: PGM Representation**](/material/#topic-2-pgm-representation)
3.  [**Topic 3: PGM Inference**](/material/#topic-3-pgm-inference)
4.  [**Topic 4: PGM Learning**](/material/#topic-4-pgm-learning)
5.  [**Topic 5: Autoregressive Models**](/material/#topic-5-autoregressive-models)
6.  [**Topic 6: Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)**](/material/#topic-6-variational-autoencoder)
7.  [**Topic 7: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**](/material/#topic-7-gan)
8.  [**Topic 8: Normalizing Flows**](/material/#topic-8-normalizing-flow)
